# The Illusion of Free Will — Who Is Really in Control?

A 50-second surreal, philosophical motion-graphics film (16:9, 4K, 30 fps).

- **`freewill-4k.mp4`** (3840×2160) · **`freewill-1080p.mp4`** (1920×1080)
- **`index.html`**: the live, real-time version. Space plays or pauses, ←/→ seek, and `?t=25` starts at a given second.
- **`render.cjs`**: frame-exact renderer (headless Chromium → ffmpeg)

| Time | Scene | Imagery |
|---|---|---|
| 0–9 s | **I · The Question of Choice** | A lone backlit figure stands in a dreamlike landscape of purple and orange clouds, with a low sun, god rays and floating wireframe polyhedra. Hundreds of luminous pathways branch ahead of the figure, splitting and reconnecting while pulses travel along them. The camera glides into the figure's head. |
| 9–19 s | **II · Inside the Human Mind** | A flight through a 3D neural universe of 1,500 neurons and their connections, coloured electric blue, magenta and gold. Synchronized waves of activity spread through the network as signals travel along its connections. A small readout shows the readiness potential (Libet et al., 1983), labelled as an interpretation that is still debated. |
| 19–29 s | **III · The Illusion of Control** | An impossible room with floating clocks running at different speeds (some backwards), drifting mirrors, suspended doors and an upside-down staircase. The figure walks to a blue door and a red door and reaches out, and the moment fractures into overlapping translucent versions that reach for different doors. |
| 29–40 s | **IV · The Divided Self** | A profile face emerges from crimson darkness and splits into two facing selves. One is filled with reason (grids, a golden spiral, a rotating icosahedron and equations), the other with emotion (drifting colour fields and flowing curves). They merge into one luminous figure as a network grows out around it. |
| 40–50 s | **V · The Mystery of Free Will** | The camera pulls back on the floating figure, surrounded by golden rays, turquoise waves, rings of sacred geometry and pathways reaching out in every direction. The figure dissolves into particles that re-form as a giant question mark, and the film fades to darkness. |

Free will is presented as an **open** scientific and philosophical question.
